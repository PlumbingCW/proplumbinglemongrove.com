Leak detection is the process of finding where water escapes a pipe without tearing out walls to look. Active leaks are an emergency. If water is spreading or the meter keeps turning with every fixture off, close the main valve and call our 24/7 line. We schedule same-day and after-hours leak checks when a plumber is available.

Slow leaks are quieter and cost more over time. A pinhole that weeps for six months can soak insulation, lift flooring and feed mold behind the wall. Finding it early limits the repair to a few square feet.

When a hidden leak is an emergency

Call at once if you hear water running with everything off, see a wet patch growing on a ceiling or floor, or find warm spots on a slab. A hot spot on the floor can mean a hot water line is leaking beneath it.

Close the main valve to stop the loss. If the house is still without a break in the leak, open a low tap to drain the lines and call us. Most hidden leaks can wait a few hours for a plumber once the main is closed, which is why we start with a controlled shutoff.

Is the leak inside the house or outside?

Start with the water meter. Turn off every fixture and look at the leak indicator, the small triangle or dial that spins when water flows. Movement means water is leaving somewhere. Then close the main valve to the house. If the indicator stops, the leak is inside. If it keeps moving, the leak is between the meter and the house.

Your water bill carries the same clue. Helix Water District supplies Lemon Grove, so a jump on your bill with no change in habits is a reason to run this test. We can do it for you in a few minutes.

How we find a leak

1

Meter and pressure test

We isolate the supply line and watch for pressure loss to learn whether the leak is on the supply side.

2

Acoustic listening

Sensitive ground and wall microphones pick up the sound of water escaping a pressurized pipe.

3

Thermal imaging

An infrared camera shows temperature differences from a leaking hot line or wet material.

4

Moisture mapping

A moisture meter marks the edges of the wet area so we cut only where needed.

Signs of a hidden leak

Higher bills

A rise with no change in habits is the earliest warning, and the easiest to check.

Musty smell

Odor without visible water suggests moisture behind a wall or under a floor.

Bubbling paint

Wall paint that blisters or peels from the inside often sits over a wet cavity.

Low pressure

A leak on the supply line can lower pressure at fixtures.

Codes, permits and licensing

Work on leak detection in California is governed by the California Plumbing Code, a state version of the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Whether a permit applies depends on the scope, so ask the City of Lemon Grove building department or ask us before work begins.

Plumbing contractors in California hold a C-36 license from the Contractors State License Board, and you can check any license at cslb.ca.gov.
