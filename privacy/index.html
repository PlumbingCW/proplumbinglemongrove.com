Privacy Policy

How we handle information from calls and from this website.

24/7 emergency line
(619) 927-0312
Call now

This policy explains what Lemon Grove Pro Plumbing collects when you use this site or call our line. It is written for a call-based service. Our site has no forms and no accounts.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your name, the address of the job and details about the plumbing problem so we can book and perform the work.

Website information

Like most sites, ours may log basic technical data such as your browser type, the pages you view and the time of your visit. We use this to keep the site working and to understand which pages are useful. Maps on our pages are provided by Google and may set their own cookies under Google's privacy policy.

How we use information

  • To schedule and perform plumbing work you request.
  • To answer questions about a job or an invoice.
  • To improve our service and train our staff.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations.

Sharing

We share information with the plumbers and partners who perform your job and with service providers who help us run the business. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to legal requirements. Call or email us using the details in the footer.
