Lemon Grove Pro Plumbing is based at 7220 Broadway in Lemon Grove, and the 24/7 line dispatches to the city and the neighboring East County communities. This page shows where we go, how the places relate to each other and which ZIP codes we use.

Drive times below are estimates and change with traffic. Emergency dispatch depends on where a plumber is when you call, so we confirm the arrival plan by phone.

Lemon Grove, our home base

Lemon Grove covers 3.88 square miles, sits about nine miles from downtown San Diego and has more than 27,000 residents. Our Lemon Grove ZIP codes are 91945 and 91946. State Routes 94 and 125 serve the city, and the Orange Line trolley stops at the Lemon Grove Depot and Massachusetts Avenue stations.

Most Lemon Grove calls are a short drive from Broadway. For what we see in local homes, start with drain cleaning, water heater repair and leak detection.

La Mesa, next door

La Mesa borders Lemon Grove on the northeast and has its own page, emergency plumber La Mesa, covering ZIP codes 91941 and 91942, the Village, Grossmont and Mount Helix.

Spring Valley, El Cajon and the communities around them

Spring Valley lies east of Lemon Grove and uses ZIP codes 91977, 91978 and 91979 among others. El Cajon sits farther east and uses 92019, 92020, 92021 and 92022. Helix Water District serves both, along with Lemon Grove and La Mesa.

Smaller communities such as La Presa and Casa de Oro fall within this area as well, and our emergency line covers them. They do not have their own pages, because their plumbing issues match their neighbors'.

Water and emergency services across the area

Helix Water District supplies water to Lemon Grove, La Mesa, El Cajon and unincorporated areas including Spring Valley and Lakeside. Heartland Fire and Rescue serves Lemon Grove, La Mesa and El Cajon. If a leak threatens an electrical panel or you smell gas, call 911 first. Work inside the property line follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

For sewer questions, your sewer provider depends on your address. The City of Lemon Grove public works office can tell you who serves yours.

How older housing affects the calls

Lemon Grove was subdivided in 1892, and La Mesa's first large subdivisions came between 1906 and 1912, so the area has many older homes. Older homes mean older drain lines, older shutoff valves and water heaters that have been replaced once or twice already. When we arrive we look at the whole system, not only the fixture that failed.

Newer construction nearby has its own pattern: tighter layouts, tankless units and fixtures that need specific parts. The service pages explain how we approach each type of job, from drain cleaning to repiping.

How to confirm you are in range

The simplest test is a phone call. Give the street address, and we will tell you whether we can send a plumber and about how long the trip may take. Traffic on State Route 94 and State Route 125 changes the answer through the day, which is why we do not publish fixed arrival times.

For planned work, such as a water heater installation or a fixture installation, we can usually schedule a window that suits you. Emergencies go to the front of the line.

Who to call for what

Gas leaks, downed power lines and flooding that reaches electrical equipment are emergencies for 911 or your utility. Water shutoff at the street, billing questions and water quality reports go to your water district, which for much of this area is Helix Water District. Inside your property line, from the meter to the fixtures, is plumbing work, and that is the part we handle.

What to do while you wait for a plumber

  • Close the main water valve if water is flowing where it should not.
  • Stop using drains if a sewer line is backing up.
  • Keep people and pets away from standing water near outlets.
  • Write down when the problem began and what you have already tried.

What we do across the whole area

Whatever the town, we handle the same work: emergency calls such as burst pipes and sewer backups, water heater work, drains, leaks and planned projects. See the full list on our plumbing services page.
