A burst pipe is an emergency, and speed matters more than anything else. Close the main water shutoff, switch off the breaker for any wet room if you can reach the panel dry, and call our 24/7 line. Same-day and after-hours repairs are scheduled whenever a plumber is available.

Burst pipe repair means stopping the flow, cutting out the failed section and replacing it with sound pipe and proper fittings. We also look at why it failed, because the same cause often threatens the next stretch of pipe.

What to do when a pipe bursts

1

Shut off the main

The main valve is usually at the front of the house, near the meter, or on the garage wall. Turn it fully clockwise. A quarter-turn ball valve closes with a quarter turn.

2

Drain the lines

Open the lowest tap in the house and flush a toilet once to empty the system and reduce spraying.

3

Cut power to wet areas

If water is near outlets, switch off those breakers only when the panel and floor around it are dry.

4

Call and document

Call us, then photograph the damage and move valuables. Your insurer will ask for photos.

If you cannot find the main shutoff, say so when you call. We can guide you to it by phone and tell you what to look for at the meter.

Why pipes burst in San Diego County

Freezing is not the usual cause here. Lemon Grove sits at about 446 feet above sea level, and freezing nights are rare. Bursts and pinholes more often come from corrosion, high water pressure, worn fittings and age.

Older homes in Lemon Grove may still have galvanized steel that rusts from the inside, or thin-wall copper that develops pinholes after decades of use. High street pressure speeds both. A pressure regulator that has failed raises the stress on every joint in the house.

Signs a pipe is about to fail

Stains on walls or ceilings

A brown ring that grows after you run water points to a leak inside the wall or above the ceiling.

Rust-colored water

Galvanized pipe corroding from the inside often shows up first as discolored water at the tap.

Hissing or running sounds

Water moving when every fixture is off means a leak between the meter and the house.

Sudden pressure drop

A burst in the main line or a branch will pull pressure down at every fixture.

How we repair a burst pipe

1

Stop the water

We confirm the main is off and relieve pressure in the line before cutting.

2

Find the failure

We expose the damaged section and look at the pipe on both sides for corrosion that will fail next.

3

Replace the section

We cut back to sound pipe and replace it with copper or PEX, using fittings suited to what is already in the wall.

4

Test and restore

We bring the system back to pressure and check every joint and the walls nearby for moisture.

Insurance often covers sudden water damage to floors and walls from a burst pipe. The pipe itself and gradual leaks are usually excluded. Call your insurer promptly and keep our invoice.

Prevent the next burst

Learn where your main shutoff is before you need it, and exercise it twice a year so it does not seize. Ask us to test your water pressure. A reading above the range your fixtures are rated for justifies a new regulator.

Watch for stains and sounds, and book leak detection when a water bill jumps without a reason.

Codes, permits and licensing

For burst pipe repair, the standard to ask about is the California Plumbing Code, which builds on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Permit needs vary by job, and the local building department is the place to confirm them.

Plumbing contractors in California hold a C-36 license from the Contractors State License Board, and you can check any license at cslb.ca.gov.
