Call now

The fastest way to reach us is to call. Our line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for emergencies and for planned work. There is no contact form on this site, and we do not answer plumbing emergencies by email.

When you call, have the address, a short description of the problem and anything you have already shut off. We will ask three or four questions and then book the first available plumber. Same-day appointments are offered when available.

Before you call

Emergency checklist before you call

  • Leak or flood: close the main water valve clockwise.
  • Sewer backup: stop all water use and do not flush.
  • Gas smell: leave the building and call 911 or your gas utility first.
  • Electrical hazard near water: stay away and call 911 if anyone is at risk.
