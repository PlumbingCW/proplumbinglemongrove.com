Water heater installation is the work of placing a new heater where none stood before, or relocating one during a remodel, and connecting it to water, fuel, venting and a drain. A well-done install includes a relief valve with a proper discharge pipe, shutoff valves, a pan where needed and clear access for the next person who services it.

We install tank heaters in gas, electric and hybrid heat pump types. For planned installs we can talk through options by phone, then confirm details at the site.

Decisions to make before installation

Fuel

Gas gives faster recovery. Electric is simpler to vent. Heat pump units save energy and need space and airflow.

Size

Match capacity to how many people use hot water at the same time, not to the size of the home.

Location

Garages, closets and utility rooms all work, but clearances and venting differ.

Accessories

A pan, an expansion tank and seismic straps may be needed depending on setup.

Where the heater goes

Gas units need combustion air and a vent path to the outside, with clearances from combustibles. Garage installs come with their own placement rules, so ask the City of Lemon Grove building department what applies to your address.

A closet install needs enough space for service and a vented door for combustion air. We measure first so the new unit actually fits.

Lemon Grove notes

Lemon Grove is a city of about 27,000 people covering 3.88 square miles, and its homes range from older bungalows to newer builds. That mix means a garage install in one house and a closet install in the next. We plan for what your house actually has.

Helix Water District supplies the city. For sediment questions, ask Helix for its annual water quality report instead of relying on rules of thumb. We set the maintenance advice to your water once you share what the report says.

Codes, permits and licensing

Plumbing in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). For water heater installation, materials, venting and connections should meet that code, and larger jobs may need a permit from the City of Lemon Grove building department.

Plumbing contractors in California hold a C-36 license from the Contractors State License Board, and you can check any license at cslb.ca.gov.
