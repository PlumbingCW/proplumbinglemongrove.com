A sewer camera inspection is a look inside the sewer line using a waterproof camera on a flexible cable. The camera travels from a cleanout toward the street while a monitor and recorder capture what it sees. You see roots, cracks, offsets, bellies and buildup the way we do, instead of guessing from symptoms.

It is the best first step when a line clogs repeatedly, before buying a house, before a remodel that adds fixtures, and before any digging. A short recording often saves the cost of repairing the wrong thing.

When to book a sewer camera inspection

Before you buy

A home inspection does not look inside the sewer line. A camera does, and the recording helps you negotiate.

Repeat clogs

If cabling clears a line and it clogs again, the camera shows why.

Before a remodel

Adding a bathroom adds load. The camera shows whether the line can take it.

After a backup

It confirms the line is sound after a cleaning, or shows what needs repair.

If sewage is backing up right now, that is an emergency, and our emergency sewer line repair team can inspect as part of the visit. Planned inspections are booked on the schedule.

Lemon Grove notes

Lemon Grove grew from a citrus town into a suburb across the 20th century, which means its sewer laterals date from many different periods. A camera tells you what yours is made of, and what condition it is in.

If you are buying in Lemon Grove or nearby La Mesa, ask whether the seller has a recent camera recording. If not, you can order one during the inspection period. Sewer laterals on private property are generally the owner's responsibility, so the recording matters at the closing table.

Codes, permits and licensing

For sewer camera inspection, the standard to ask about is the California Plumbing Code, which builds on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Permit needs vary by job, and the local building department is the place to confirm them.

Plumbing contractors in California hold a C-36 license from the Contractors State License Board, and you can check any license at cslb.ca.gov.
