The water service line, or water line, is the buried pipe that brings water from the meter at the street into your house. A break there is an emergency. You may see a flooded yard, hear water running or lose pressure at every tap. Close the house shutoff and call our 24/7 line. Same-day and after-hours visits are scheduled when a plumber is available.

Water line repair patches a failed section. Water line replacement swaps the entire run, by digging a trench or by pulling a new pipe through the old path without opening the whole yard.

Broken water line: what to do right now

1

Close the house valve

This valve is at the house, often by a hose bib or in the garage. It stops water from the house side.

2

Check the meter

If the meter still turns with the house valve closed, the leak is in the line before it. Close the meter valve only if you have a key or a wrench made for it.

3

Keep people away from soft ground

Wet soil above a trench can give way. Keep children off the area.

4

Call and photograph

Take photos of the wet ground and the meter reading for your records.

If the break is on the utility side of the meter, the water district handles it. Helix Water District serves Lemon Grove, so call Helix for a leak between the main and the meter. Between the meter and the house, the line is generally the owner's.

Signs of a failing water line

Low pressure everywhere

A leaking or corroded service line cannot carry full flow to the house.

Wet patch with no rain

A damp or sunken spot in the yard along the pipe path.

Rusty or cloudy water

Old steel service lines corrode from within and release rust.

High bill

A leak between meter and house pays for itself on the bill.

How we find and fix the break

1

Test the line

We isolate the line and watch pressure to learn whether it holds.

2

Pinpoint the leak

Acoustic equipment on the ground narrows the spot.

3

Expose and repair

We dig only as needed, cut back to sound pipe and join it with fittings made for buried service.

4

Backfill and test

The trench is closed in layers and the line is pressure tested.

Lemon Grove notes

Lemon Grove covers 3.88 square miles, and the service lines under its older streets were laid at different times. Steel, copper and plastic all turn up. Ask us which pipe your house has, because that decides the repair.

Permits for digging in the street or the parkway are handled by the City of Lemon Grove, so we ask the city which permits apply to your address. We do not guess at those rules.

Codes, permits and licensing

Plumbing in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). For water line repair, materials, venting and connections should meet that code, and larger jobs may need a permit from the City of Lemon Grove building department.

Plumbing contractors in California hold a C-36 license from the Contractors State License Board, and you can check any license at cslb.ca.gov.
