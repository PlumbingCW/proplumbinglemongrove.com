A water softener reduces the minerals, mainly calcium and magnesium, that make water hard. A whole-house filter removes particles, chlorine taste or other items depending on its media. They solve different problems, so the first step is learning what is in your water.

We install softeners, sediment and carbon filters and plan the plumbing around them. We do not tell you what your water contains without a test or a utility report.

Test before you buy

Hardness varies by water source, and it is the figure that matters when sizing a softener. We do not state a hardness number for Lemon Grove here. Helix Water District supplies the city and publishes an annual water quality report, which is the right place to look.

You can also test your tap with a home kit, or send a sample to a certified lab. Write down the hardness in grains per gallon, the chlorine level and any other flagged items, and bring them to us.

Signs of hard water

White crust on faucets, spots on glass, soap that will not lather and scale in kettles.

Signs of a filter need

Chlorine taste or smell, sediment in water or cloudy water.

What neither fixes

Pressure problems, pipe corrosion and lead. Test for what you suspect.

Water for drinking

If taste is the concern, a point-of-use filter at the kitchen tap may be enough.

Lemon Grove notes

Many Lemon Grove homes sit in older blocks with garages that hold the heater and the main line. That makes the garage the natural place for a softener. A tight garage may limit the tank size, so we measure first.

If you rent, a softener is a landlord decision. Nearly half of Lemon Grove homes are rented, so ask the owner before you buy equipment.

Codes, permits and licensing

For water softener and filtration, the standard to ask about is the California Plumbing Code, which builds on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Permit needs vary by job, and the local building department is the place to confirm them.

Plumbing contractors in California hold a C-36 license from the Contractors State License Board, and you can check any license at cslb.ca.gov.
