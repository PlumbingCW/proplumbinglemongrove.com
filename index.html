When to call an emergency plumber in Lemon Grove

An emergency plumber should answer the phone at 2 a.m. and tell you what to shut off. Here is what to do first, whatever the problem.

1

Shut off the water

For any leak, close the main valve clockwise. It is usually at the meter, the front of the house or the garage wall.

2

Stop using drains

For a backup, no sinks, showers, laundry or flushing until the line is clear.

3

Stay safe near electricity

Keep away from wet outlets and panels. Switch off breakers only if the area around them is dry.

4

Call the 24/7 line

Local knowledge

Plumbing across Lemon Grove

Lemon Grove covers 3.88 square miles about nine miles from downtown San Diego. What is under your floor depends on the block.

Older homes, mixed pipes

Lemon Grove was subdivided in 1892 and filled with lemon trees before suburbs replaced them. Homes of many ages share each block, so galvanized steel, copper, cast iron, clay and plastic all turn up. We start every visit by learning what your house has, because the pipe decides the repair. See sewer camera inspection for a look inside the drain line.

Broadway and the business corridor

Broadway is the city's main commercial road, with Lemon Grove Plaza and Country Plaza along it. Restaurants and shops there depend on grease lines and restrooms that must work during open hours. We schedule commercial plumbing calls around your business hours.

Water from Helix

Helix Water District serves Lemon Grove, La Mesa and El Cajon, and publishes an annual water quality report. We do not quote hardness here. If your heater or fixtures scale up, check the report or test your tap, then ask about softeners and filters.

Renters and landlords

Nearly half of Lemon Grove homes are rented, based on 2020 Census figures. Report a leak or backup to the landlord in writing, take photos and keep the invoice if you call us. Quick action limits the damage and protects everyone.
