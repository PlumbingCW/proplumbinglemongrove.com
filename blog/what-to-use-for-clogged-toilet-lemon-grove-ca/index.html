What to Use for a Clogged Toilet in Lemon Grove, CA

Most toilet clogs are small, and the right tool clears them in a minute. Here is what to reach for first, and what to leave alone.

A clogged toilet is one of the most common household plumbing problems, and most clogs sit in the toilet's own trap, right below the bowl. That makes them easy to reach. The right tool and a calm approach clear most of them without a plumber.

Start by stopping the flush. If the bowl is rising, take the lid off the tank and push the flapper down to cut off more water. Then pick your tool.

The tools, in the order to try them

Tools for a clogged toilet
ToolBest forNotes
Flange plungerMost clogsHas a rubber flap that fits the drain opening

Closet augerStubborn clogsA short cable with a protected tip for toilets

Using a plunger the right way

1

Choose a flange plunger

A flat cup plunger is for sinks. The flange fits the toilet drain.

2

Seat it fully

Push the plunger into the drain opening so the cup covers it and the flap fills the hole.

3

Push and pull

Push gently first to avoid splashing, then pull up sharply. Repeat ten to fifteen times.

4

Test the flush

If the water drains, flush once. If it rises, stop and move to the next tool.

Try hot water and dish soap

Squirt a generous amount of dish soap into the bowl and let it sit for ten minutes. Pour a bucket of hot tap water from waist height. Do not use boiling water, because it can crack porcelain.

When to use a closet auger

A closet auger is a rigid tube with a cable inside. Feed the tip into the drain until you feel resistance, then crank the handle to break up or hook the clog. The tube keeps the cable from scratching the bowl.

If the auger reaches a hard object, such as a toy or a bar of soap, you may pull it out. Gentle pulling is better than force.

Call a plumber before the water rises.

What to avoid

Chemical drain cleaners

They can burn skin, damage old pipes and leave caustic water for the plumber.

Boiling water

It can crack the bowl.

Repeated flushing

Every flush adds water that may overflow.

Flushable wipes

They are marked flushable but clump in pipes and cause clogs.

Signs the problem is bigger than the toilet

If a plunger clears the toilet and it clogs again within a day, or if the tub or sink gurgles when you flush, the clog is farther along. A main line blockage will not clear with a toilet tool.

In that case stop using water and call. See sewer line cleaning for how we open a main line, and toilet repair for the fixture itself.

If the water is already rising

Do not flush again. Lift the tank lid and press the flapper down so no more water enters the bowl, or turn the shutoff valve behind the toilet clockwise. Towels around the base give you a few extra minutes.

If the bowl is only half full, you have room to plunge. If it is about to overflow, dip some water out into a bucket first. A bucket and an old cup are better than a mop and an apology.

Protect the floor before you plunge

Lay old towels or a plastic sheet around the toilet. Wear rubber gloves. Keep the plunger in a bucket so it does not drip on the way to the sink. After the clog clears, rinse the plunger in the toilet and clean it with a household disinfectant.

What to do with a lost object

A toy, a cotton swab or a brush that slipped in can lodge in the trap. Stop flushing. A closet auger may snag it, or you can reach in with a gloved hand if the object is near. If it will not move, call a plumber. A toilet that has to come off the floor to retrieve an object is a job for someone who can reset it with a new wax ring.

Codes and licensing to know about

Plumbing in California is governed by the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Whether a project like this needs a permit depends on the scope, so ask the City of Lemon Grove building department before you start. Contractors hold a C-36 plumbing license from the Contractors State License Board, which you can verify at cslb.ca.gov.

Plumbing in Lemon Grove: local notes

Who is responsible for a backup depends on where the blockage sits. Within the house and on the private lateral, the owner generally is. The City of Lemon Grove public works office can tell you who maintains the public main for your street. If you are unsure, call us and we will help you find out.

A running water bill matters too. Helix Water District supplies Lemon Grove, and a toilet that runs after every flush adds to it. A toilet leaking into the bowl wastes water without a sound.
