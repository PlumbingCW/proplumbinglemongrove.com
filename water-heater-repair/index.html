A water heater that stops heating or starts leaking needs attention fast. Our 24/7 line takes water heater repair calls day and night, and we schedule same-day and after-hours visits when a plumber is free. Water heater repair means finding the failed part, such as a thermostat, heating element, gas valve or relief valve, and fixing it if the tank is still sound.

While you wait, find the shutoff on the cold water pipe above the tank and turn it clockwise. For a gas unit, set the gas control to pilot or off. For an electric unit, switch off its breaker before anyone touches the panel.

When a water heater problem is an emergency

Water pooling around the base, a hissing or popping noise, a rotten egg smell near a gas heater, or a relief valve that is discharging all deserve a same-day call. A small seep from a fitting is repairable. Water seeping from the tank shell means the steel has failed and repair will not hold.

If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Do not flip switches or light anything. Heartland Fire and Rescue provides fire and rescue service to Lemon Grove, La Mesa and El Cajon.

Shut it down safely

  1. Turn the cold water supply valve above the heater clockwise until it stops.
  2. Gas heater: turn the control knob to off. Electric heater: switch off its breaker.
  3. Open a hot tap in the house to relieve pressure.
  4. Put a bucket under any drip and call us.

What fails on a water heater

No hot water, gas

A failed thermocouple, gas control valve or dirty burner assembly is the usual cause. These are common repairs.

No hot water, electric

A tripped breaker, a failed upper or lower element, or a bad thermostat. A meter test tells us which.

Lukewarm water

Sediment buried the lower element or burner, or a dip tube broke and cold water is mixing at the top.

Dripping from the valve

The temperature and pressure relief valve may be worn, or pressure in the house is too high. Both need checking.

Where sediment is heavy, a yearly flush and an anode rod check extend tank life. If your heater sits in a garage, a loose seismic strap or a corroded gas flex line is worth fixing while we are there.

Codes, permits and licensing

Work on water heater repair in California is governed by the California Plumbing Code, a state version of the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Whether a permit applies depends on the scope, so ask the City of Lemon Grove building department or ask us before work begins.

Plumbing contractors in California hold a C-36 license from the Contractors State License Board, and you can check any license at cslb.ca.gov.
