Drain cleaning means removing whatever blocks a pipe so wastewater can leave the house again. That can be hair and soap in a tub, grease in a kitchen line, or tree roots in the main sewer line. The tool changes with the cause, and so does the price.

When a clogged drain is an emergency

Not every slow drain needs a midnight call. Treat it as urgent when more than one fixture is affected, when a toilet gurgles after the shower runs, or when sewage smells rise from a floor drain. Those signs point to the main line, not a single trap.

While you wait for the plumber, stop using water, pull rugs and boxes away from the lowest drain in the house, and do not pour chemical drain cleaner into a standing backup. Chemicals can burn skin and make the job riskier for the person who has to open the pipe.

Do this first

  • Stop all water use, including the dishwasher and washing machine.
  • Do not flush. Wastewater has nowhere to go.
  • Keep children and pets out of wet areas near a backed-up floor drain.
  • Take a phone photo of the backup in case a landlord or insurer asks for it.

Why drains clog in Lemon Grove homes

Lemon Grove was subdivided in 1892 and described as a sea of lemon trees by 1894, and the city grew to 19,348 residents by 1960. Homes of very different ages sit on the same street, so the pipes behind your walls may be cast iron, clay, plastic or a mix of all three.

Older cast iron and clay lines roughen with age and snag grease and paper, while mature trees in older yards send roots into joints. Newer plastic lines clog less from roughness but still catch wipes and kitchen grease. Knowing the age of your house tells us where to look first.

Roughly half of Lemon Grove homes are rented, based on the 2020 Census. Tenants should report a backup to the landlord in writing and call a plumber when a landlord cannot be reached, because water damage spreads quickly and the paper trail protects everyone.

Signs of a clog before it becomes a backup

Slow single drain

Usually hair, soap or grease in the trap or the pipe just past it. A snake from the nearest access point normally clears it.

Gurgling pipes

Air is being pushed through a partial blockage. Gurgling from more than one fixture suggests the main line.

Water in a second fixture

Running the washer and seeing the shower drain bubble means the line they share is restricted.

Repeat clogs

A drain that clogs again within weeks has a pipe problem, such as a belly, a root or a broken joint, not just debris.

How we clear a drain

1

Locate the blockage

We ask which fixtures are slow, then test them to learn whether the clog is in one branch or the main line.

2

Choose the tool

A hand auger suits a sink trap. A motorized cable machine handles longer runs. Roots and heavy grease call for jetting.

3

Clear and flush

After the cable breaks the clog, we run water to confirm full flow and flush debris out of the pipe.

4

Inspect if needed

If the clog returns or the cable hits something solid, a camera shows the cause so you can decide on repairs.

Keeping drains clear between visits

Put a hair catcher in every shower, wipe greasy pans into the trash, and keep wipes out of the toilet even when the package says flushable. Run hot water for a minute after washing dishes by hand.

If the main line has clogged twice, ask about a scheduled sewer line cleaning. A cleaning on your schedule costs less than an emergency visit at night.

Codes, permits and licensing

Plumbing in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). For drain cleaning, materials, venting and connections should meet that code, and larger jobs may need a permit from the City of Lemon Grove building department.

Plumbing contractors in California hold a C-36 license from the Contractors State License Board, and you can check any license at cslb.ca.gov.
